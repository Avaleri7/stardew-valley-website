About the Game

Stardew Valley is a farming simulation game where players move to a small town and start a new life. Players can grow crops, take care of animals, explore caves, fish, and build relationships with the people in the town.

Welcome to the valley! There is always something new to discover.

Responsive Design Reflection

  1. The most significant layout issue I discovered was that the page needed a flexible content area and images that could fit smaller screens. Without responsive rules, content can feel crowded or extend beyond the screen.
  2. I used a centered container with a maximum width, flexible width, and consistent box sizing. I also used margin and padding to separate sections, and set images to a maximum width of 100% with automatic height so their proportions stay correct.
  3. At a wide viewport, the content has more space and the navigation links can sit in one row. At a narrow viewport, the media query reduces spacing and lets the navigation links wrap, making the page easier to use on a phone.
  4. Testing at different widths helped me notice that a layout can look fine on a computer but feel cramped on a phone. It also reminded me to check that images stay inside the content area and that text wraps naturally without horizontal scrolling.
  5. Next, I would improve the design by testing more devices and making the navigation even easier to use with touch controls.
